About Me

The Mind Behind the Ideas. The Creativity Behind the Brands. I'm Kemi Esther Kareem, a creative strategist, digital marketer, and the founder of KE Creatives, based in Lagos, Nigeria. I've always been drawn to creativity and the possibilities that come with turning an idea into something people can see, feel, and connect with. For me, creativity goes beyond making things look beautiful. It's about understanding a vision, finding the right way to communicate it, and bringing it to life in a way that resonates with the right audience.

One thing about me? Give me an idea, tell me about your brand, and my mind is already working. I'm naturally curious about possibilities. I start thinking about the story we could tell, the content we could create, the visuals that would capture attention, and the strategies that could help your brand stand out. Sometimes, one conversation is all it takes to get the ideas flowing.

That passion is what inspired me to build KE CREATIVES: a space where creativity meets strategy to help businesses and personal brands communicate their value, build a stronger online presence, and connect meaningfully with their audiences. From developing content ideas and designing captivating visuals to creating engaging videos and planning social media strategies, I approach every project with fresh ideas, attention to detail, and a genuine desire to understand the brand behind the brief. I believe every brand has a story worth telling. My job is to help you tell yours creatively, strategically, and in a way that makes people pay attention.

"You bring the vision. I bring the ideas, creativity, and strategy to help bring it to life."

Let's create something meaningful together.
